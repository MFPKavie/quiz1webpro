Food From My Hometown

Here are some local foods from Malang.

Bakso

Bakso is a traditional Indonesian meatball soup. It consists of meatballs made from beef.

Where to try it
Bakso Cak Toha

Rawon

Rawon is a traditional Indonesian beef soup with a distinctive dark color from its kluwek.

Where to try it
Rawon Nguling

Martabak

Martabak is a popular local dessert (terangbulan) or snack, often served with various fillings. My personal favorite combination is chocolate and cheese.

Where to try it
Martabak Madonna
